Red Wolf Radio adds your own music to Workers & Resources: Soviet Republic through Republic Mod Loader.

Version 1.0.0 requires Republic Mod Loader 1.0.1 and supports the verified Workers & Resources 1.1.1.9 binaries on Windows x64. Launch the game through RML. Enable only one copy of Red Wolf Radio.

Play WAV, MP3, FLAC and RIFF XWMA/WMAudio2 from your Windows Music folder or a folder you choose. Folders are scanned on every launch, with optional plugin-local Music. Copy XWMA music from other Workshop items into your music folder to use it here. No tracks or filenames are hardcoded, and album art is ignored.

XWMA uses the game's music loader without conversion to WAV. The supported WMAudio2 profile is mono/stereo, 16-bit decoded samples, 8–48 kHz. Long or Unicode source paths use an unchanged compressed cache copy. WAV/MP3/FLAC use compatible cached WAV files. Keep all, selected or none of the original soundtrack. Game files and source music are preserved.

In a loaded republic, open Settings → Other → Scripts (the 0101 icon), select Red Wolf Radio, and confirm with the green checkmark. One compact, draggable player shows the source filename without extension and refreshes automatically when the song changes. Its 360×128 UI-unit frame scales with the game. The four icon buttons are Previous, Restart, Play/Pause and Next; hover over an icon to see its action name in the title bar. Previous goes directly to the preceding track. Closing the window leaves music playing. Reopening starts it in the center; its position is not saved.

The player is enabled by default with [Display] NativeSongPopup=true. The separate five-second title notification is optional and off by default: ShowTrackName=true enables it. Language=ru selects Russian hover descriptions and messages; filenames are unchanged. Add or edit these keys in the existing Display section.

During gameplay: Ctrl+Shift+Right for Next; Ctrl+Shift+Left to restart, then a quick second press for Previous; Ctrl+Shift+Down to pause/resume. Shortcuts are configurable and work while the game is the foreground application. Track navigation preserves a user pause. Shortcuts are inactive on the main menu.

Defaults prepare five upcoming tracks, protect three previous tracks, and use a 4 GiB soft cache limit. Protected tracks may exceed the limit. Up to 64 playback entries are remembered during a launch. No music is included. English and Russian INI help and documentation are included.

Edit plugin/RedWolfRadio.ini for folders, original exclusions, cache settings, shortcuts, the player and the optional title notification. Back up your customized INI outside Steam and game folders before any update, including ordinary Workshop updates. The 1.0 download replaced it with the shipped template, although the earlier 0.9.0 test had retained it. Check settings afterward and restore your backup with the game closed if needed.
