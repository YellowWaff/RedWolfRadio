Red Wolf Radio adds your own music to Workers & Resources: Soviet Republic through Republic Mod Loader.

Version 0.9.0 requires Republic Mod Loader 1.0.1 and is verified with Workers & Resources: Soviet Republic 1.1.1.9 on Windows x64. Launch the game through Republic Mod Loader. Compatibility checks restrict music hooks to the verified game and loader binaries.

Play WAV, MP3 and FLAC files from your Windows Music folder or a folder you choose. Folders are scanned on every launch. An optional Music folder beside the plugin DLL is also supported. Keep all, selected, or none of the original soundtrack; original game files are preserved. Original game XWMA music is supported. External XWMA files are not supported in this build.

Custom audio is prepared as WAV in a shared cache. Defaults prepare five upcoming tracks, protect three previous tracks, and use a 4 GiB soft cache limit. Protected tracks can temporarily exceed that limit. No personal songs are included.

In a loaded republic, use Ctrl+Shift+Right for Next, Ctrl+Shift+Left for Previous, and Ctrl+Shift+Down for pause/resume. Previous first restarts the current song; a quick second press goes back. Controls work while paused. Up to 64 playback entries are remembered during a launch; three previous entries are protected in the cache by default. Shortcuts do not operate in the main menu.

Edit plugin/RedWolfRadio.ini to change source folders, original-track exclusions, cache settings and keyboard shortcuts. Ordinary Steam Workshop updates preserve customized settings. Keep an external backup before RML force-refresh, unsubscribe/resubscribe, or manual replacement because those workflows may recreate the item folder.
